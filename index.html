The Balance

You can't let an AI just do things to your cloud.
And you can't keep watching every alert.

That tension is exactly what Chandra is built for. Most "AI ops" tools either watch but never act, or act on their own and leave you cleaning up. Chandra does neither — it observes, flags what matters, and only changes things when you say so.

Without Chandra

  • Alerts you can't keep up with, real and noise mixed together
  • Cloud waste discovered weeks after it starts
  • Slow, manual change-control reviews for every fix
  • No clear record of what got changed, or why
  • Audits pull findings you can't easily satisfy

With Chandra

  • A daily briefing that separates what matters from noise
  • Five health domains watched in one place
  • Risky changes wait for your approval — read-only until then
  • A record of the steps taken for each task
  • A governed, auditable trail for compliance reviews

Governance & Safety

Restraint is the feature. The audit trail is the proof.

For teams that must change AWS safely under audit, Chandra's discipline is its value.

Human-in-the-loop

Consequential change is gated: review the plan, then approve, reject, or escalate.

Verified fixes

After approval, a fix is verified to have actually landed — not just assumed done.
